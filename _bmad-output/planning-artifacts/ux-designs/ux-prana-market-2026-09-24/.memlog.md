- (fact) Prana Market: E-commerce platform for cosmetics and beauty in Colombia with hybrid sourcing (local physical + Mastershop dropshipping).
- (fact) Currency & payments: All pricing in COP. Primary gateway Wompi (Nequi Push/QR, PSE, Bancolombia, Cards, Cash vouchers) + Stripe backup.
- (decision) Stack & Foundation: Next.js 16 App Router, Tailwind CSS v4, Lucide Icons, Framer Motion, mobile-first responsive web.
- (direction) Visual identity: Warm beauty luxury accessible to Colombian consumers — soft rose, warm nude, clean editorial typography, high contrast WCAG AA.
- (decision) Core UX Surfaces: Storefront Home, Catalog PLP with origin badges, PDP with shade selector and delivery estimator, Persistent Slide-over Cart with COP 85k free shipping bar, Nequi-optimized Checkout, Tracking Portal.
- (decision) Color Palette: Option 1 Rose & Champagne Luxury (#D45B7A primary dusty rose, #C89D66 champagne gold accent, #FDF9F7 silk background, #2D1B22 espresso typography, #E8839B rose highlight, #FCE7ED subtle tint).
- (decision) Hybrid Cart Shipping UX: Option A full transparency. Explicit badges (Envío Exprés Local vs Dropshipping Nacional) and separate delivery estimate cards when cart contains mixed items.
- (decision) Mobile PDP Ergonomics: Sticky bottom bar with shade/variant quick selector, Añadir al Carrito, and direct Pagar con Nequi button.
